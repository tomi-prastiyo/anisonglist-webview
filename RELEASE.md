## 🎵 English (Release Notes)

### Overview
Welcome to the initial public release of **AniSongList** for Android!
AniSongList is a dedicated, high-performance WebView application for exploring, listening to, tracking, and rating thousands of anime opening (OP) and ending (ED) themes.

### ✨ What's New in v1.0.0:
- **Dedicated Multimedia Engine**: Unrestricted JavaScript, DOM storage, and hardware acceleration enabling high-definition anime video and audio streaming.
- **Smart Navigation History**: Native integration with the Android system back button to traverse browsing history before prompting exit.
- **Offline Error Screen**: Automatically detects connection losses and presents an interactive retry screen with one-tap recovery.
- **Cloudflare & Turnstile Compatibility**: Modern mobile user-agent configuration preventing bot false-positives and ensuring instantaneous SPA hydration.
- **Official Branding**: Integrated high-resolution AniSongList adaptive icons across all Android display densities.
- **Lightweight & Privacy-Focused**: Direct connection to official AniSongList servers without analytics overhead or bloat.

### 📥 Download & Installation
1. Download **`AniSongList-v1.0.0.apk`**.
2. Open the downloaded file on your Android device (ensure *Install from unknown sources* is allowed for your browser/file manager).
3. Tap **Install** and enjoy AniSongList!
